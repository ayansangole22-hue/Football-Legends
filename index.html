KNOW THE GAME

⚽ RULES OF SOCCER

Learn the rules that keep the beautiful game fair, exciting and competitive.

⏱️

01. Match Duration

A standard adult match lasts 90 minutes: two halves of 45 minutes. Half-time is normally up to 15 minutes. The referee allows for stoppages, and extra time may be played in some knockout matches.

👥

02. Players

Each team can have up to 11 players on the field, including one goalkeeper. A match cannot start or continue if either team has fewer than seven players.

🥅

03. Scoring a Goal

The whole ball must cross the goal line between the posts and beneath the crossbar. The goal counts provided no offence has occurred. The team with more goals wins.

🚩

04. Offside

Being in an offside position alone is not an offence. At the moment a teammate plays or touches the ball, an attacker must be level with or behind the second-last opponent, or level with the ball, to avoid an offside position. An offence occurs if the attacker becomes actively involved in play from an offside position. There is no offside offence directly from a goal kick, throw-in or corner kick.

🟨

05. Yellow Card

A yellow card is a caution. It may be shown for unsporting behaviour, dissent or repeatedly breaking the rules. Two yellow cards in the same match result in a red card.

🟥

06. Red Card

A red card means a player or team official is sent off. Serious foul play, violent conduct and certain other offences can result in a red card. In standard association football, a sent-off player cannot be replaced.

🧤

07. Goalkeeper

Inside their own penalty area, a goalkeeper may handle the ball with their hands, subject to the Laws of the Game. They generally cannot handle a ball deliberately kicked to them by a teammate or received directly from a teammate's throw-in.

🦵

08. Fouls & Free Kicks

Tripping, pushing, holding or tackling an opponent carelessly, recklessly or with excessive force can result in a foul. Depending on the offence, the referee awards a direct or indirect free kick, or a penalty kick.

🎯

09. Penalty Kick

A penalty is awarded for certain direct-free-kick offences committed by a defending player inside their own penalty area. The kick is taken from the penalty mark, 11 metres from the goal line.

↩️

10. Throw-In

When the whole ball crosses a touchline, the opponents of the player who last touched it take a throw-in. The thrower uses both hands to deliver the ball from behind and over the head, following the required foot-position rules.

🚩

11. Corner Kick

A corner kick is awarded when the whole ball crosses the goal line, last touched by a defending player, without a goal being scored. The attacking team takes the kick from the appropriate corner area.

🧑‍⚖️

12. The Referee

The referee enforces the Laws of the Game, manages disciplinary action, controls restarts and keeps track of match time. Players must respect the referee's decisions during the match.

⚽ REMEMBER: These are summaries of the standard Laws of Association Football. Competition regulations may vary, and the referee applies the Laws during the match.
THE JOURNEY

MOMENTS THAT MADE HISTORY

Different eras. Different heroes. One beautiful game.

1958 — Pelé Arrives

At just 17, Pelé helped Brazil win the World Cup and announced himself on the world stage.

1986 — Maradona's Masterpiece

Maradona inspired Argentina to World Cup glory with one of the most celebrated individual tournaments in history.

2002 — Brazil's Golden Five

Ronaldo led Brazil's attack as the Seleção won their fifth men's FIFA World Cup.

2022 — Messi Completes the Dream

Argentina won the World Cup in Qatar, giving Messi the biggest international trophy of his career.
